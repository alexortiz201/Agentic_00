# 🎓 Coaching -- developing an engineer's judgment

How to teach this work, as distinct from how to do it.

Coaching is also one of the declared engagement modes, and the mode does not relax any rule: everything produced under it meets the same authority, gates and evidence discipline as delivery work. **What changes is who decides and who explains, not what is permitted.**

## What belongs here

Teaching method, and only teaching method. The loop a session runs, how to ask rather than answer, how mastery is judged from evidence, and what a session leaves behind.

The *subject* being taught does not live here. A curriculum of agentic concepts belongs in [`Agentic_Engineering/`](../Agentic_Engineering/README.md); the method for teaching any of it belongs here. The seam is worth holding: the method transfers to teaching anything, and keeping it separate is what makes it reusable.

**No person is named here.** Like the rest of `foundations/`, this area is standalone -- it describes how to coach, not who is being coached. A specific engineer's history, level and next exercise are run-local notes, not discipline.
