# Coaching Session

Record of one coaching-mode engagement. Coaching develops the engineer's judgement; it does not relax delivery rules. Everything produced in the session is still subject to the same authority envelope, gates and evidence discipline as `delivery` — the difference is who decides and who explains, not what is permitted.

The rubric, the loop and the eight persistence fields are policy; this file is only the record. Where this file quotes policy wording it is marked as such — do not reword those rows, because a locally-improved wording is how two records stop comparing. Score **from recorded evidence**, not from impression: an unscorable step is scored `0` with the missing evidence named, never rounded up because the conversation went well.

Field 5 is the one most often skipped. **"Nothing worth extracting yet" is a legitimate and complete answer**, and recording it is what keeps reuse earned rather than assumed.

## Mastery

Score 0–4 for the named competency, **only from the evidence recorded above**.

| Score | Meaning (policy wording) | Evidence required to award it |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Unfamiliar | — |
| 1 | Explains | Correct account of the mechanism, prompted |
| 2 | Applies with guidance | Completed during the session with correction |
| 3 | Independently operates and diagnoses | Completed unaided, **including diagnosing a failure** — operating without diagnosing is still a 2 |
| 4 | Transfers or improves a proven primitive | A correct transfer to a new case, or a measured improvement to something already proven |

**Do not inflate.** Never invent historical scores. Never infer mastery from a fluent explanation, from a folder existing, or from a future lesson title. Never average away weak verification or unsafe authority — one unsafe authority decision is not offset by four good ones, and a session containing it does not score above 2 regardless of the rest.
